red peonies

Peonies are one of the most magnificent spring ornaments in gardens with their huge, fragrant petals. They can live for decades in the same sunny, nutrient-rich spot without needing to be transplanted. Because of their rich variety of shapes and vibrant colors, they are a primary symbol of happiness and romance.

red tulips blooming

Red tulips are one of the most beloved heralds of spring gardens and flower beds with their vibrant, rounded petals. They prefer sunny spots with well-draining soil, where they quickly come into bloom in the spring warmth. Traditionally, they are regarded as a symbol of deep, sincere love and passion.

purple lavender field

Lavender is one of the most popular ornaments in summer gardens with its distinct purple flowers and pleasant, soothing fragrance. It prefers drier, sunny locations and well-draining soil, where it blooms abundantly year after year. For centuries, it has been used in everyday life as a symbol of peace, purity, and tranquility.

About flowers

The world of flowers is fascinatingly rich and complex. There are more than 300,000 flowering plant species on Earth, ranging in size from tiny water plants barely one millimeter long to flower structures several meters tall. Their special colors and fragrance do not just delight our eyes, but also help pollinating insects navigate, often with ultraviolet patterns that only animals can see. Throughout history, flowers have also become important tools for human communication: in the Victorian era, for example, a complete language of flowers developed to convey secret messages. Thanks to their extraordinary adaptability, they are capable of blooming almost anywhere from hot deserts to high alpine rocks, serving as symbols of life and renewal for thousands of years.
